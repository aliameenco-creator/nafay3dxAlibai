Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

1. Who we are

Ali Baii Official ("we", "us", "our") is an AI agency that builds custom websites, automation systems, and digital marketing engines for mid-market brands. Our website is located at alibaii.com.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: aliameen.co@gmail.com

2. What information we collect

Information you provide

When you fill out our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name (optional)
  • The nature of your inquiry
  • Your message

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • Your IP address (for rate limiting and security)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page (if analytics are enabled)
  • Referral source

Cookies

We use a single cookie (cookies-ok) to remember that you have accepted this notice, and a cookie (theme) to remember your dark/light mode preference. No tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies are set.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Send you information you have requested
  • Communicate with you about potential or ongoing projects

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How we store and protect your data

Contact form submissions are transmitted via encrypted email (TLS) and stored in our email inbox. We do not retain form data on any third-party database. Email communications are stored securely with Google Workspace standard protections.

We retain your contact information only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or maintain an active business relationship.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, email us at aliameen.co@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Third-party services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — to load typefaces. Google may collect basic request data. See Google's privacy policy.

If Google Analytics is enabled, it will be disclosed here along with opt-out instructions.

7. Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email aliameen.co@gmail.com and we'll reply within one working day.
