02 / Process

Five steps. No theatre.

/01

Listen

Two calls, a shared doc, and a real audit of what's actually broken — not a pitch deck dressed as discovery.

/02

Map

We sketch the system. What ships in v1, what waits, what gets killed. You see it before we touch a keyboard.

/03

Make

Design and engineering happen in the same room (Slack channel). You see daily progress, not monthly reveals.

/04

Measure

We instrument everything. If we can't show it moved, we didn't ship it. Reports in plain English.

/05

Hand-off

You get the keys, the docs, the loom walkthroughs, and a 30-day window to break things and ask anything.

09 / Questions

Things people ask first.

What does Ali Baii Official actually do?

+

We design and build custom websites, portfolio sites, automation systems, and AI workflows — plus the digital marketing engines that make them earn their keep. One studio, four disciplines, no hand-offs between vendors.

Who do you work with?

+

Mid-market brands that have outgrown templates and want something engineered for them — usually $5M–$200M revenue. Founders, CMOs, and operators who want fewer vendors and more outcomes.

How long does a project take?

+

A custom site lands in 4–7 weeks. Automation builds run in 2–4 week sprints. Marketing engines are ongoing — we don't believe in 12-month "transformation" projects.

Do you keep the code or do we own it?

+

You own everything. Code, copy, accounts, automations. We hand over keys, not leases.

How do payments work?

+

50% to start, 50% on hand-off for fixed projects. Retainers are billed monthly, pause-anytime after month three. Wire, ACH, or card.

What if AI isn't the right answer?

+

We'll tell you. We've turned down work where the real fix was a sales script, a hire, or a better landing page. Honesty is a service, too.
